About

Way to Live is a free, independent manual on looks, skills, philosophy and psychology. It exists for one reason: most people drift through their days without ever deciding what they want from them.

What you'll find here

  • Short lessons across five areas: Lookmaxxing, Skillmaxxing, Philomaxxing, Psymaxxing and Lifemaxxing.
  • The Social Spin, a random daily task for building real-world confidence.
  • A Quote of the Day drawn from a shelf of 60. Where a quote's source is uncertain, we say so.

Our approach

Everything on this site is written and reviewed by hand. The content is general information for personal growth. It is not medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice, so please talk to a qualified professional about your own situation.

Funding

The site is supported by advertising, which is clearly labeled and kept separate from our content.
